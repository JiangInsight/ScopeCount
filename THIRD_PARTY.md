# Third-party software and sources

Builders obtain DuckDB 1.5.5 (MIT) and CRoaring 5.5.5 (Apache-2.0) from their pinned official releases. The original [CRoaring license](research/artifact/engine/CRoaring-LICENSE) is preserved. Downloaded headers/libraries retain upstream notices and are not committed as project source.

Apache Arrow/PyArrow is Apache-2.0 licensed. Python dependencies are pinned in `requirements.txt`; their licenses remain with their distributions.

External workload programs, authors' repositories, releases, and data-use links are identified in [docs/data.md](docs/data.md). The original traffic-stop and FCC programs are external sources and are not distributed here as project code.
